How it works

From the first tap to your whole group on the map in about a minute.

1

Start a ride

Enter a riding name, create a ride and share the 6-digit code or link. No password.

2

Friends join

Riders enter the code or tap your link. The leader starts the ride when everyone is ready.

3

See your tribe

A live map and a simple list show ahead, behind, stopped or no signal. Get an alert when someone stops.

Help, one tap away

Hold the SOS button and your location goes to every rider in your ride. Six quick messages need no typing: Fuel stop, Puncture, Slow down, Regroup, Break, All good.

  • Save up to 5 emergency contacts. After an SOS you get one-tap Call and "Text my location" buttons. You tap; nothing is sent for you.
  • Throttle Tribe does not detect crashes and does not contact emergency services. In India, dial 112.
  • It needs mobile data to share locations. With no signal, your updates reach the group when you are back online.

Private by design

  • Your location is shared only with riders in your current ride.
  • Sharing runs only while a ride is open. It stops when the ride ends or you leave.
  • We do not upload your phone contacts. No ads, no selling your data.
  • Delete your account and data any time.

Installing on Android

Throttle Tribe is a brand-new app from a new developer, so it comes as a signed APK from GitHub Releases rather than the Play Store (that comes later). Android shows a couple of extra prompts for apps installed this way. That is normal.

Step by step
  1. Tap Download for Android above and open throttle-tribe.apk when it finishes.
  2. If Android says it can't install from this source, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source (also called "Install unknown apps") for your browser or Files app. Go back and tap Install.
  3. If Google Play Protect says it hasn't seen this app or developer before, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open Throttle Tribe, pick a riding name and allow location when asked.
Why does Play Protect warn me?

Play Protect shows this notice for any app from a developer it hasn't seen before. It does not mean it found a problem. The release page lists the APK's SHA-256 checksum so you can check that your download matches.

After installing

You can switch "Install unknown apps" back off for your browser in Settings. Updates are published on the same releases page.

On iPhone: the web app

There is no iPhone app yet. You can join and ride using the web app in Safari.

Know the limits

  • It shares your location only while the page is open and the screen is on. If you lock your phone or switch apps, your group stops seeing you.
  • Keep Safari open on the ride page, with the phone mounted and powered.
  • Android and iPhone riders can be in the same ride.
