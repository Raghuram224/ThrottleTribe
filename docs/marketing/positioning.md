# Throttle Tribe: positioning

Status: draft for product review. Based on what the app can do today (see "Not claimed" at the end).

## One-liner

**See your whole riding group on one live map: who's ahead, who's behind, who's stopped, and who needs help.**

Short form (store, bio): "Keep your tribe together." Tagline: "Ride together. Stay together."

## Elevator pitch

Group rides fall apart in small ways: someone stops for fuel and the group rides on, someone drops
out of signal and nobody notices, someone has a puncture and has to call around. Throttle Tribe fixes
that. One rider creates a ride and shares a 6-digit code. Everyone joins in seconds with just a
riding name, no sign-up. During the ride, the map and a simple rider list show who is ahead, behind,
stopped or out of signal. If someone stops for longer than you chose (1, 3 or 5 minutes), the group
gets an alert. If someone needs help, one tap sends an SOS to everyone in the ride. Six quick
messages (Fuel stop, Puncture, Slow down, Regroup, Break, All good) replace typing on the bike.

## Audience

| Segment | Who | What they care about |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Weekend and touring riding groups in India (friends, office groups, club chapters; 4-20 riders) | Not losing riders on highways and ghats; regrouping without 10 phone calls |
| Secondary | Ride leaders and sweepers of clubs and events | Knowing who is where, who has stopped, when to wait |
| Tertiary | Pairs and couples riding two bikes | Simple, no-setup location sharing for one trip |

Context: mixed Android/iPhone groups, gloves, sunlight, handlebar mounts, patchy mobile data in
hills and tunnels. English first; Hindi and regional languages later.

## Top 3 benefits

1. **Nobody gets left behind.** Live map plus ahead / behind / stopped / no signal at a glance.
2. **Know early.** Stop alerts after the time you choose, and a clear "no signal" state that is never confused with "stopped".
3. **Help in one tap.** SOS with your location goes to every rider in the ride. Quick messages need no typing.

## Differentiators

| Differentiator | Why it matters | Backed by |
|---|---|---|
| Built around "ahead / behind / stopped / lost signal", not just dots on a map | Answers the 3 questions riders actually ask | Rider list, status states, distance along the leader's path |
| "No signal is not stopped" | Fewer false alarms in tunnels and hills | Status logic (offline after 60 s without an update, never inferred as stopped) |
| No sign-up: join as a guest with a name | Group members join in under a minute | Guest sign-in, 6-digit code or link |
| Location only for your ride, only while the ride is open | Trust and privacy | Private ride channel; sharing stops at end or leave |
| Glanceable, glove-friendly UI | Safer to use on the move | 56 px touch targets, high contrast, dark mode |
| Cross-platform | Mixed phone groups just work | Android + iOS |

## Proof points (use only these)

- Join with a 6-digit code or link; no password.
- 6 quick messages: Fuel stop, Puncture, Slow down, Regroup, Break, All good.
- Stop alert time selectable: 1, 3 or 5 minutes.
- Background location runs only during a ride, shown by a persistent notification on Android.
- Free on Android and iOS (confirm pricing before launch).

Do not invent: user counts, ride counts, awards, "trusted by", or testimonials until real ones exist.

## Brand voice

Rider-to-rider, short, warm, confident, plain English. In-ride copy is glanceable (4 words or fewer
where possible). SOS and alert copy states the fact and the next action; no panic, no blame.
Words we use: ride, tribe, riders, leader, regroup, stop. Words we avoid: geolocation, realtime
sync, telemetry, "guaranteed", "never miss".

## Not claimed (truthfulness guardrails)

Throttle Tribe does NOT: detect crashes; contact emergency services; work without mobile data;
provide offline maps; offer group chat or free-text messages; plan routes or navigate. SOS reaches
riders in the same ride only. Never imply otherwise in any channel, including social posts and
influencer briefs.
